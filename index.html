    HOW TO PLAY

    Learn the basics of 184. Secret combinations are yours to discover.

    01
    THE GOAL

    Each player starts with 100 HP. Defeat the AI by reducing its HP to 0, or have more HP when the cards run out.

    02
    YOUR DECK

    Each player has 30 cards: Hammer, Paper and Scissors numbered 0 through 9. You start with five cards.

    03
    PLACE YOUR CARDS

    Drag a card from your hand into a numbered slot. Drag it back to your hand, move it between slots or swap two placed cards before confirming your turn.

    04
    SUIT ADVANTAGE

    📄 Paper beats ✊ Hammer · ✊ Hammer beats ✂️ Scissors · ✂️ Scissors beats 📄 Paper.

    05
    DAMAGE

    Damage equals the difference between the two card numbers. The higher number wins a same-suit clash. When different suits share a number, the winning suit deals twice that number.

    06
    WINNING A ROUND

    Win at least two of the three slots to win the round. Win all three to inflict five bonus damage. If you win one slot, lose one and tie one, whoever dealt more total damage wins the round; if all three slots tie, both players choose a card to discard. In endgame rounds with fewer slots, win more slots than the opponent.

    07
    DISCARD & DRAW

    The round winner chooses one played card to discard. The loser discards one at random. Remaining played cards return to hand; normally each player draws one card next round.

    08
    TIME & FINAL ROUNDS

    You have 75 seconds to place cards. A slot you leave empty when time runs out stays empty. An enemy card facing an empty slot hits your HP directly (a 0 hits for 10). Play no card at all and a random card from your hand is also discarded. Discard selection has a 50-second limit. When a hand has fewer than 3 cards, that player simply plays fewer cards and may use any open slot; discarded cards never return.

    🗑 YOUR DISCARD PILE

    These are your permanently discarded cards. The discard pile is never reshuffled. Once all playable cards are gone, the player with more HP wins.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Each player has 30 unique Hammer, Paper and Scissors cards numbered 0–9 and starts at 100 HP.
  2. Drag from hand to any open slot; move cards back or swap between slots. You can also tap cards and slots.
  3. Paper beats Hammer, Hammer beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper; with identical suits, the larger number wins.
  4. Damage is the number difference. Different suits with equal numbers: winning suit deals double that number.
  5. Win at least 2 of 3 slots to win the round; if fewer slots are active, win more than the opponent. Sweep all 3 for 5 bonus damage.
  6. Winner chooses one played card to discard. Losers/ties discard randomly. Other played cards return.
  7. Normally draw 1 new card at the start of the next round. Special combos are secret.
  8. Reduce enemy HP to 0 or finish with higher HP. Quitting forfeits the match.
